# 07. The Impossible Trinity: The Official Promise Is a Free Put Option

> **⚡ Transferable rule:** **Pick two of three, and pay the price honestly — in advance.** A fixed peg plus domestic rates above abroad equals a free put option handed to speculators; don't wait for the crisis to start choosing.

## 📖 What the book actually says

- **The physical law:** capital mobility, independent monetary policy, and a fixed exchange rate cannot coexist. This is a constraint to be paid for, not a puzzle to be outsmarted.
- **The lethal moral hazard:** a country holding a peg while running domestic rates above foreign rates is **issuing free insurance to the entire market**. Firms conclude "the state will never allow depreciation" and borrow unhedged short-term foreign currency without a second thought. **The central bank, with finite reserves, underwrites unlimited downside FX risk for every private actor — for free.**
- **Three defense paths, each cutting meat:**

| Path | Action | Direct cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Defend the rate** | Violent rate hikes / drain liquidity (HK overnight rate hit **280%** on 1997-10-23) | "Seven wounds fist": mortgage rates spike, property freezes, leveraged developers break; equities crash on discount-rate and margin-call cascades |
| **Capital controls** | Close the conversion window, restrict withdrawals | Official rate becomes fiction, offshore black-market discount soars; FDI dies, letters of credit bounce. **You trap the speculator — and the real economy with him** |
| **Float** | Let the currency go | Baht fell ~50% in months; local-currency value of dollar debts explodes; mass bankruptcies |

- **The endgame requires state-level action, not private heroics:** when Korea's reserves ran dry in late 1997, it was the Fed and the IMF convening the international banks to impose a **debt standstill** — rolling hundreds of billions in short-term bank claims into sovereign-guaranteed medium-term bonds. Only sovereign credit, bundling dispersed private claims and cutting off indiscriminate collection, buys the real economy time to breathe.

> *"Subsidies and controls can suppress prices; they cannot suppress risk. A suppressed signal doesn't disappear — it detours into the shadow pool."*
> *"The trilemma is not a technical puzzle that policy wisdom can route around; it is a physical constraint paid in social cost."*

## 💡 How it rewired my decisions

- **Before:** in the "import boutique with three promises" wargame (free conversion + locked rate + my own pricing), I picked closing the gates — capital controls as a clever shell game, trapping speculators' tokens inside.
- **After:** pushed 72 hours past the closure: the official counter still says 1 token = 100, but with withdrawals frozen a street discount (50, then 30) appears instantly and the official rate is fiction. "Enter freely, exit never" destroys commercial trust — customers stop topping up, suppliers demand cash. **You saved the cash number in the warehouse and paralyzed the whole commercial network.**
- **The distinction that survived:** separate micro self-rescue from macro systemic moves. Individuals auctioning everything for dollars just pay the whole system's bill alone (and rationing blocks them anyway). Sovereign-level debt standstill converts "my default" into "a bargaining game between creditors and a state" — when thousands of debtors pull the steering wheel off at once, **the banks holding the claims become the panicked side.**
